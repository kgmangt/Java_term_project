## My Final Term Project: Weapon Enhancement Game
To be honest, my Java skills are still a work in progress, but I wanted to challenge myself to build a game from scratch. This project is a final requirement for my course, but I believe it will be a valuable stepping stone for my future as a developer. I know there will be plenty of hurdles along the way, but I'm excited to see it through to the end!

### 🎮 Game Explanation
The concept is simple: **Enhance your weapons, sell them, and stack your profits!**
* **The Risks:** The higher the enhancement level, the lower your success rate.
* **The Rewards:** Use the money you've earned from your successful enhancements to buy chance up!

#### Goal
Keep pushing your luck until you forge the ultimate, "legendary" weapon at level 12!
Can you discover what lies beyond Level 12? Keep upgrading to unveil the mystery!

### 🕹️ How to Play
1. Enter your name and age
2. Click the **Game Start** button
3. Click **Enhance** to upgrade your weapon
4. If enhancement fails, your weapon returns to level 0
5. Click **Sell** to sell your weapon and earn gold
6. Click **Chance Up (500,000 gold)** to increase your success rate
7. Reach level 12 to clear the game!

### ⚙️ How to Run
```cmd
cd Java_term_project
javac EnhanceWeapon.java
java EnhanceWeapon
```
